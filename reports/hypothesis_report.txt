  ─ Construct-validity note on the sensitivity result ─────────────
  Barrier count is confounded by engagement: respondents who never
  attempted to measure cannot report barriers. In this sample, the
  14 respondent(s) endorsing 'no barriers' show higher do_breadth (mdn=0)
  than the 74 reporting barriers (mdn=1) – the opposite of the
  theoretical prediction. The primary can_effort measure avoids
  this confound by asking directly about perceived worthwhileness.

  → H5 VERDICT:  SUPPORTED ✓  primary ρ=+0.417 (p<0.001)
